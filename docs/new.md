# [Brief] ETHDenver 2024

:::info
:bulb: This document details the planning around [ETH Denver 2024](https://www.ethdenver.com/), background, and necessary steps before attending.
:::

## :chart_with_upwards_trend: Objective

:::success
The objective of this project is to spread awareness of HackMD within the web3 industry and build relationships with key industry leaders. 
:::

### :small_blue_diamond: Main target:
1. Meet with DAOs, startups, communities, and investors to spread the word of HackMD and convert key target audiences.
2. Offer X months free to key target audiences.
3. Announce newly launched crypto payments (if applicable).

### :small_blue_diamond: Tactical elements
- [Track attending companies / communities](https://hackmd.io/2NMXXhDjT0-q9bTK4JocYg#Attending-companies)
- Set up meetings with ideal partners
- [Track ancillary events](https://hackmd.io/2NMXXhDjT0-q9bTK4JocYg#Ancillary-events)
- [Announce that we'll be attending](https://blog.hackmd.io/blog/2024/02/20/see-you-at-ethdenver) (blog, social, newsletter)
- [Determine budget needed](https://hackmd.io/2NMXXhDjT0-q9bTK4JocYg#-Budget-planning) (flights, hotels, swag)
- [Create landing page for signup](https://hackmd.io/prime/ethdenver) 
- [Create landing page brief for team](https://hackmd.io/vSbAnY2XTf6lfpczh-G9xQ)
- [Create business card QR code for signups](https://linear.app/hackmd-product/issue/D-291/determine-design-for-qr-cards) 
- Determine if there are any PR opps while on the ground (panels, podcast recordings, Twitter spaces, etc)

### :small_blue_diamond: Target audience:
- Occupation: DAO leader, web3 executive, developer
- Age range: 25-50
- Pain points: Building virtually, lack of documentation and organization

## :pencil: Content & goals 🎯

:::success
By and large, our content around the event will streamline efforts on the ground, frictionlessly helping new users learn about HackMD and educating potential partners on why they should work with HackMD. Goals will level up to the primary objective of spreading awareness.  
:::

### :small_blue_diamond: Content categories

1. Pre-event
    - Blog announcement, social, and newsletter
2.  On the ground
    - Landing page
    - QR cards
    - Crypto payments announcement blog and social (if applicable)
3. Post-event
    - Blog recap, social, and newsletter
